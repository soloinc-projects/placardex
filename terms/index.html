What Placardex is, and is not

Placardex is a reference to the U.S. Hazardous Materials Table, 49 CFR §172.101, with its Appendices A and B, and to the segregation table of 49 CFR §177.848. It shows the regulation’s text. It does not classify a shipment or approve it, and it does not give emergency response guidance.

Reference only — verify against the official 49 CFR text and your company’s own HazMat training/certification before shipping.

Every entry shows the edition of the text it comes from, so you can check it against the official text. If you think a value is wrong, email support@8086.llc.
